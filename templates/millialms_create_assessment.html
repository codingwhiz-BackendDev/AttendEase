Quick tips

  • True/False: pick the correct choice from the dropdown.
  • Fill blank: list accepted answers, one per line (case-insensitive).
  • Short answer & Essay: auto-flagged as Pending for manual grading with feedback.
  • Set points per question. Total is the sum of all questions.
